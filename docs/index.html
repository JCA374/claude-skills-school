The one idea to remember

A skill is a folder with a SKILL.md file. Claude doesn't read your skills up front. It reads only their descriptions, picks the one that fits your request, and only then loads the instructions, and later the scripts. That's why the description is the trigger, and why a skill can be long without costing anything until it's used.
