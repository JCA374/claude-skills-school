# Checklist: empirical studies with statistics

- **Sample size and power.** Is n stated for every analysis? Was a power calculation done before the study? Small
  samples give large, unstable effects.
- **Multiple comparisons.** How many outcomes, subgroups and models were tested? Twenty tests at p < 0.05 give one
  "significant" result by chance. Look for a correction or a pre-registration.
- **Pre-registration.** Do the primary outcomes match the registered ones? A switched outcome is a major issue.
- **Effect size, not just p.** Is the effect large enough to matter in practice? Is there a confidence interval?
- **Confounders.** For observational data: what else differs between the groups? Is causation claimed from
  correlation?
- **Missing data and drop-out.** How many were lost, and were they different from those who stayed?
- **Analysis matches design.** Paired data analysed as paired, clustered data (classes, hospitals) with clustering.
- **Figures.** Truncated y-axes, cherry-picked time windows, error bars that aren't defined.
