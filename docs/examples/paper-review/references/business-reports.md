# Checklist: business, consultancy and policy reports

- **Who paid, who benefits?** Note the commissioner and whether the conclusion favours them. Not a flaw by itself,
  but it raises the bar for evidence.
- **Sources of numbers.** Every market size, growth rate or saving should have a source and a year. Circular
  sourcing (report A cites B, which cites A) is common.
- **Survey quality.** Sample size, who was asked, response rate, question wording. "80% of executives" out of 25
  self-selected respondents is weak.
- **Projections.** What assumptions drive the forecast? Is there a sensitivity analysis or a range, or one number?
- **Case studies.** Are they chosen to show success only? Ask what happened in the cases that failed.
- **Recommendations follow from findings?** Each recommendation should trace back to a finding in the report.
- **Executive summary vs body.** Does the summary claim more than the analysis supports?
